## 📌 Executive Summary

The **ASEAN-GCC Trade & Healthcare Governance Observatory** is an institutional-grade decision support platform designed to analyze macro-level healthcare governance, public spending baselines, and cross-border regulatory friction across ASEAN, the Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC), and strategic transit corridors.

By integrating real-time indicator feeds directly from the **World Bank Open Data REST API**, this observatory transforms raw macro data into actionable risk metrics for Corporate Boards, Risk Committees, and Policy Strategists.

---

## 🚀 Key Features & Architectural Highlights

1. **Real-Time World Bank API Pipeline:** Automated data ingestion filtering historical expenditure indicators (`SH.XPD.CHEX.GD.ZS`).
2. **Interactive Executive Command Center:** Live country, region, and timeline filtering with reactive Plotly visual engines.
3. **Macro Shock & Stress Testing Engine:** Scenario modeling tool allowing decision-makers to simulate GDP contraction and inflation shocks on cross-border capital exposure.
4. **Multi-Corridor Radar Index:** 4-dimensional governance clustering comparing ASEAN vs. GCC structural stability and volatility.
5. **Predictive Analytics Module:** Historical trend modeling projecting spending baselines up to 2030.
6. **Institutional Data Export:** Authenticated CSV data extraction for secondary econometric research.
